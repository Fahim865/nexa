# Adobe Stock Prep guideline-checker sources

The local checker is advisory only. It does not claim Adobe approval or determine legal clearance.

## Deterministic technical checks

- JPEG output is expected for photos and illustrations.
- The image colour space should be sRGB.
- Image resolution must fall between 4 MP and 100 MP.
- File size must not exceed 45 MB.
- Watermarks, timestamps, branding, visible logos, and identifiable rights concerns require human review and cannot be reliably decided by a browser-only checker.

## Metadata guidance

- Titles should be brief, clear, accurate, and ideally under 70 characters.
- A submission can include up to 49 keywords.
- The first 10 keywords carry the greatest search importance.
- Keywords should be relevant, non-duplicated, use one account language, and exclude trademarks, brand names, and personal information.
